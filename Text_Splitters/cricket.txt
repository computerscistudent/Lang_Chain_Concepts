The Canvas of Leather and Willow
The morning sun begins its slow ascend,
On emerald fields where shadows stretch and bend.
The dewdrop glistens on the blades of green,
The quietest morning that you've ever seen.

The heavy roller makes its lonely rounds,
Preparing battle on these sacred grounds.
The umpire steps out, clad in pristine white,
As nervous tension grips the morning light.

The skipper looks up at the spinning coin,
Two eager openers prepare to join.
A nervous shuffle and a tightened strap,
They walk out to a gentle, roaring clap.

The bowling spearhead marks his running track,
With thoughts of sending stumps and bails aback.
He storms in, pounding hard against the clay,
And with a fierce release, begins the day.

The red leather shines, it swings across the air,
The batsman leaves it with an inch to spare.
The keeper gathers with a heavy thud,
The battle line is drawn upon the mud.

Then comes the change, a subtle, spinning trick,
The ball bites hard, the turning pitch is quick.
A sudden loop, a leap, a desperate dive,
The slip cordon is suddenly alive!

"How's that?!" rings out, a fierce, collective cry,
Beneath the vast and burning summer sky.
The finger raises slowly to the air,
One side finds joy, the other finds despair.

The midday heat gives way to evening breeze,
As shadows stretch beneath the distant trees.
The stadium lights blink open, bright and grand,
Illuminating thousands in the stand.

The game is down to just a final few,
The asking rate has climbed into the blue.
A modern gladiator takes his stance,
To give his sinking team a fighting chance.

He dances down, the willow meets the ball,
A sound that echoes clear against the wall.
It clears the boundary, sailing through the night,
A glorious, majestic, soaring flight.

Two runs required, and just a single ball,
The final showdown to decide it all.
The bowler charges, giving all his might,
The batsman swings into the blinding light.

A scamper, and a dive, the dust arose,
As drama meets its cinematic close.
For win or lose, when stumps are pulled away,
The handshakes prove that friendship won the day.

The game dissolves into the twilight gleam,
The timeless magic of the willow's dream.


"The Canvas of Leather and Willow" captures the essence of a cricket match from dawn to dusk, illustrating the beauty and tension of the game. 

The poem opens with a serene morning scene, where the sun rises over lush fields, setting the stage for the day's competition. 

As the players prepare, the atmosphere is filled with nervous anticipation, highlighted by the umpire's presence and the skipper's coin toss.

The action unfolds with the bowler's fierce delivery and the batsman's careful defense, showcasing the skill and strategy involved. 


The poem vividly describes the ebb and flow of the game, including moments of excitement and tension, such as appeals for dismissals and the thrill of boundary shots. 

As the match progresses into the evening, the stakes rise, culminating in a dramatic final showdown.

Ultimately, the poem emphasizes the camaraderie and sportsmanship that transcends the competition, as players shake hands regardless of the outcome. 

The closing lines reflect on the enduring magic of cricket, symbolized by the "willow" of the bat, and the timeless joy it brings to players and spectators alike.
